Help Center

What you can do in ReflexCoTI

Explore the tools available for coding transcripts and developing themes.

Watch the ReflexCoTI tutorial

Follow the complete workflow from project creation and transcript upload through coding, codebook review, and theme development.

9:20 Watch on YouTube ↗

Create research projects

Set up a project with your research question, add transcripts, and keep your study materials organized in one workspace.

Code transcripts

Highlight transcript excerpts and create your own codes, or use AI coder agents to generate suggested codes and supporting excerpts.

Review and refine AI codes

Inspect each AI-suggest codes. Accept, revise, merge, split, reject, or add additional codes so the results reflect your interpretation.

Build a codebook

Bring reviewed codes from multiple transcripts into one codebook. Revise the codebook if necessary.

Generate themes

Create themes manually or generate AI theme suggestions from the finished codebook. Connect themes to relevant codes.

Review and refine AI themes

Accept, revise, merge, split, or reject suggested themes. You can add additional themes so the results reflect your interpretation.

Collaborate with your research team

Invite researchers and share suggestions.

Export your work

Export your human coding, reviewed AI coding, and project themes for use in your research workflow.
